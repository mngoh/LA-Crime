The question

Black women are a far larger share of assault victims than women of any other group. Is that a higher rate of victimization, or just where assaults happen?

A higher rate. Per 100,000 residents a year, Black women are assaulted 1,803 times: 3 times the rate of Hispanic women, 5.4 times White women, 17 times Asian women, and more often than Hispanic or White men.

Findings

The rate gap is real

Black women's victimization rate is 1,803 per 100,000 a year. Hispanic women: 598. White women: 335. Asian women: 106. Population share does not explain it.

Closest to parity with men

Black women are assaulted at 79% of the rate of Black men. For Hispanic, White and Asian women the figure is 62%, 61% and 56%.

Simple assault is where women outnumber men

56.5% of Black simple-assault victims are women, the only group above 50%. In aggravated assault the share is 34.4%, against 24.5% to 27.1% for other groups.

South LA carries the counts, Central the rate

77th Street (2,069) and Southeast (1,658) have the most Black female victims. Central has the highest rate (7,124) on a small resident population.

Mostly strong-arm

58% of all assaults involved hands, fists or feet rather than a weapon. Handguns appear in 6,029.

Limits

Reported crimes only; reporting rates differ by group and area. Rates use residential population, so divisions with many visitors or unhoused residents read high. 8,621 victims with unknown race or sex are excluded from rates.

Method

LAPD victim descent codes mapped to four groups (Asian combines the Asian descent codes). Population by census tract from the ACS 2020 to 2024 five-year release via Census Reporter; 1,110 tracts inside the city were assigned to LAPD divisions by tract centroid using the city's division boundaries (11 fell outside). Rates divide victims by residents and by 3.5 years. Cells with fewer than 2,000 residents are not rated.

Sources: LAPD Open Data (Crime Data from 2020 to Present), US Census Bureau ACS, LA GeoHub. Reproduce with scripts/compute_rates.py and scripts/build_page.py.
